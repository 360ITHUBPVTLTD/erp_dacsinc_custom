roles & responsibilities — order lifecycle

How our teams coordinate to fulfill your orders.

An overview of the different teams involved in the order lifecycle, from quotation to manufacturing and final delivery, and their specific responsibilities.

Roles & Responsibilities

What each role is responsible for in the order lifecycle.

RoleStatusStages it coversWhat it is responsible for
Marketing Team new Lead, Quotation Create/edit Leads; create, edit, and submit Quotations.
Inside Sales new Quotation Create, edit, and submit Quotations.
Merchandiser User existing Sales Order creation & approval, Material Request, Pick List Create/edit Sales Orders and move them through the approval workflow; raise Material Requests; create and submit Pick Lists. Scoped to their own customers' orders only.
Production Team new Material Request, Subcontracting PO/Issue, Embroidery Issue Raise Material Requests; create and submit Subcontracting Orders and the Stock Entries that issue material to a jobber; create and submit embroidery work.
Purchase Team new Purchase Order creation Create and edit Purchase Orders. Approval is completed by Management.
Purchase Manager existing Purchase Order approval Standard Purchase Order access, including submit.
Inward Team new GRN/PR, Subcontracting Receipt, Embroidery Receipt, Delivery Note, Stock Transfer Create and submit Purchase Receipts and Subcontracting Receipts; log embroidery work received back; create and submit Delivery Notes; create and submit Stock Entries (covers Stock Transfer).
Accounts Team new Purchase Invoice, Delivery Note, Sales Invoice Create and submit Purchase Invoices, Delivery Notes, and Sales Invoices.
Packing Executive (Junior Merchandiser) existing Pick List Approval / Submission Create and submit Pick Lists.
