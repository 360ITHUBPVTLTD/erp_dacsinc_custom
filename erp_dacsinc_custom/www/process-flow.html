How your orders are processed and fulfilled.

01

CRM

2 stages ▸

A deal starts here, long before a Sales Order exists — a Lead moves Enquiry → Pipeline → Order, with "Lost" as a real, tracked outcome at either point along the way.

01

Lead

Marketing Team

Marketing logs the enquiry as a Lead the moment it comes in. A follow-up is scheduled and shared with whoever it's assigned to. If it goes nowhere, it's marked Lost Enquiry — never just deleted.

02

Quotation

Marketing TeamInside Sales

Drafted once the Lead is ready to be priced. Submitting it automatically moves the Lead to Pipeline. When the deal is won, creating the Sales Order directly from the Quotation automatically creates the Customer record and marks the Lead as won — moving it to the active order flow.

02

Sales

6 stages ▸

Two sign-offs before anything ships, then picking, shipping, and billing — always for exactly what was picked, never a line's full original quantity.

03

Sales Order Creation

MerchandiserMarketingInside Sales

Created from the won Quotation — this single action creates the Customer record and marks the Lead Order · WON automatically. Starts as a Draft, open for anyone to edit until it's sent for approval.

04

Sales Order Approval

Merchandiser

The Merchandiser submits the Draft for approval and, once satisfied, approves it forward to Final Approval. A reject at this point sends it back for correction, not to the front of the queue.

05

Sales Order Submission

Management

The final approval. Once management approves the order, it is officially submitted and released for fulfillment.

The moment this happens, a Pick List is created automatically for available stock. A line with no stock at all doesn't get one yet; it moves into Purchase instead until stock arrives.

06

Pick List Approval / Submission

MerchandiserPacking Executive (Junior Merchandiser)

For each line that got a Pick List, the packing team confirms the quantity. If there is some stock, they pick what is available. For any balance quantity (partially available items) or completely out-of-stock items, a Purchase process is initiated to fulfill the rest.

The Delivery Note (outlined in bold in the diagram) marks the milestone when your order physically ships. Billing and payment continue after shipping as the order is completed upon final payment.

07

Delivery Note

Accounts TeamInward Team

This is the milestone where your order physically ships. The Delivery Note is created and submitted for the shipped items. Billing is handled separately after this stage.

08

Sales Invoice

Accounts Team

Raised against that specific Delivery Note (never pulling in another line's items from the same document) — or, on the direct-billing route, raised straight from the Pick List with Update Stock, shipping and billing in one document.

Cancelling either the Invoice or the Delivery Note reopens the Pick List instead of leaving the stock stuck in limbo.

03

Purchase

11 stages ▸

Everything here starts because a specific Sales Order line is short of stock — never a general restock. Four separate shapes depending on what's actually needed: buy it, subcontract it, embroider it, or just move it.

A · Buying ready-made items or raw material▸
09

Material Request

MerchandiserProduction Team

Raised the moment a Sales Order line is short. Whether it's for the plain item itself or for a specific BOM's raw materials depends on whether that Sales Order row was built against a BOM.

If it was, it's that row's own BOM that decides the raw materials needed — never the item's current default. The same item code can be plain-bought on one order line and manufactured on another.

10

Purchase Order for Readymade & RM — creation

Purchase Team

Purchase Team raises the Purchase Order against the Material Request, for either a ready-made item or the raw material it calls for.

11

Purchase Order for Readymade & RM — approval

Purchase ManagerManagement

Signed off before it goes to the supplier.

12

GRN / PR for Readymade & Fabric

Inward Team

Inward Team records the Purchase Receipt once the shipment lands, bringing it back into available stock.

13

Purchase Invoice

Accounts Team

Accounts bills the supplier — on its own clock, independent of when the goods actually arrive. Applies the same way whether the order was a direct buy, a raw-material order, or a subcontract order below.

B · Subcontracting to a jobber▸
14

Subcontracting PO

Production Team

Raised once Production confirms the exact BOM's raw materials are available — checked against stock, any pending Material Request, and any pending Purchase Order. Short materials get their own Material Request before this can proceed.

15

Subcontracting Issue

Production Team

Raw materials are issued out to the jobber's warehouse via the Subcontracting Order and its Material Transfer.

16

Subcontracting Receipt

Inward Team

Inward Team receives the finished goods back from the jobber.

A cap on over-collection is enforced here — a jobber returning noticeably more finished pieces than were ever sent out is a real, recurring discrepancy this step is built to catch.

C · Embroidery work▸
17

Embroidery Issue

Production Team

Production sends work out for embroidery in one of three shapes: a Panel batch, a Full Piece garment, or — for smaller jobs that don't need a formal order — a plain Uniform / Embroidery Transfer.

18

Embroidery Receipt

Inward Team

Inward Team receives the embroidered pieces back. Panel work is checked and closed as its own step; Full Piece and plain transfers close as soon as they're received.

D · Stock movement▸
19

Stock Transfer

Inward Team

Inward Team's general stock-movement step — shifting material between warehouses without a formal Purchase Order behind it.
